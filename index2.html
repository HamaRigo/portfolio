Challenge

Rule-only checks missed fraud, and a stalled upstream system could stop transaction processing.

Approach

Partitioned Kafka with consumer groups, dead-letter queues and replay, plus rules combined with an ML-assisted score and Prometheus alerting.

Value delivered

More fraud caught without extra false positives, no lost transactions during upstream failures, and anomalies triaged fast from alerts instead of manual offset digging.

Highlights
  • Partitioned topics with consumer groups, dead-letter queues and replay
  • Deterministic rules combined with an ML-assisted scoring model
  • Prometheus alerting and structured logging for fast triage
